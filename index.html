Research

From fundamental quantum optics to practical quantum devices.

Our research spans fundamental and applied aspects of quantum photonics, with a particular focus on developing experimentally realizable technologies for quantum communication, sensing and computation.

Facilities

Where quantum experiments become reality.

Quantum Photonics Laboratory

Our laboratory houses optical, cryogenic, electronic and nanofabrication capabilities for the development and characterization of quantum photonic devices.

Optical Characterization

Laser systems, spectroscopy, interferometry, photon counting and time-correlated measurements.

Cryogenic Measurements

Low-temperature measurement platforms for superconducting quantum devices and single-photon detectors.

Device Characterization

High-speed electronics, time-correlated photon counting, detector metrology and nanophotonic characterization.
